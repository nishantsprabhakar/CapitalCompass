# CapitalCompass Same-Day Sale Execution Plan

Date target: July 8, 2026

Objective: create competitive buyer tension for a USD 2M minimum acquisition or strategic asset purchase of CapitalCompass, with a preferred target range of USD 5M to USD 10M if strategic value is recognized.

## Non-Negotiables

- Floor price: USD 2M.
- Preferred structure: cash asset sale or company acquisition.
- Backup structure: USD 500k to USD 1M upfront plus milestone/earn-out to USD 2M+.
- Buyer must preserve confidential-data posture and PE workflow positioning.
- Do not disclose private customer/user data, local auth store, or uploaded deal files.

## Same-Day Timeline

### Hour 0-1: Package

- Share the buyer-facing page: `docs/acquire.html`.
- Attach the acquisition teaser: `sales/01_ACQUISITION_TEASER.md`.
- Attach valuation memo: `sales/04_VALUATION_AND_DEAL_TERMS.md`.
- Keep the source repo available only after NDA or serious buyer qualification.
- Create the sale-process accounts listed in `sales/06_ACCOUNT_SETUP_RUNBOOK.md`.

### Hour 1-2: Buyer Shortlist

Prioritize 20 strategic buyers with an existing PE/M&A, private markets, data room, investment intelligence, or workflow automation product. Send Tier 1 messages first:

- Datasite / Grata
- S&P Global Market Intelligence / Capital IQ
- Morningstar / PitchBook
- AlphaSense
- SS&C Intralinks
- Intapp DealCloud
- Ansarada
- iDeals
- Diligent
- CB Insights / private markets intelligence teams

### Hour 2-4: Outbound

- Send the concise strategic buyer email.
- Send LinkedIn version to corporate development, product, or CEO/CRO profiles.
- Ask for a 20-minute same-day call.
- State that the process is time-boxed and the asset is available for strategic acquisition or exclusive licensing.

### Hour 4-6: Calls

Call agenda:

1. Problem: PE diligence remains manual, analyst-heavy, and fragmented across data rooms, documents, models, and IC memos.
2. Product: CapitalCompass ingests diligence files and produces screening notes, IC memos, models, risk scoring, audit trail, admin controls, pipeline, and monetization.
3. Strategic fit: complements data rooms, private-market databases, market intelligence, and investment workflow platforms.
4. Ask: USD 2M minimum; invite best-and-final indication today.
5. Next step: NDA, repo demo, and LOI.

### Hour 6-8: Create Pressure

- Tell interested buyers that management is prioritizing the best strategic fit and speed to close.
- Offer three paths:
  - Immediate asset purchase
  - Exclusive strategic license with purchase option
  - Acquihire/product acquisition

### End of Day

Target outputs:

- At least 10 qualified outbound messages sent
- At least 3 same-day calls requested
- At least 1 written indication of interest
- At least 1 NDA/LOI path initiated

## Deal Psychology

The USD 2M+ case is strategic, not purely revenue-multiple driven. The buyer is paying for:

- PE-specific diligence workflow IP
- Office-native output engine
- Local-first confidential processing posture
- Scoring and acquisition-readiness logic
- Admin/monetization shell
- Deal pipeline module
- Time-to-market acceleration for a platform already selling to dealmakers

## Caveat

A guaranteed same-day sale is not realistic without pre-existing buyer conversations. The highest-probability same-day outcome is a fast strategic process kickoff, NDA, demo, and written indication of interest.
