# CapitalCompass Acquisition Teaser

## Summary

CapitalCompass is a local-first private equity diligence workbench that converts company materials into PE-grade outputs: initial screening notes, diligence question banks, IC memo drafts, sponsor financial models, risk scoring, source-quality checks, deal pipeline analytics, and strategic-buyer readiness reports.

The product is built for PE funds, family offices, independent sponsors, boutique investment banks, and diligence teams that need a confidential workflow for evaluating private companies.

## Acquisition Ask

- Minimum acceptable valuation: USD 2M
- Preferred target range: USD 5M to USD 10M for a strategic buyer
- Structure: asset purchase, company acquisition, exclusive license with purchase option, or acquihire/product acquisition

## Why It Matters

Private-market diligence remains fragmented across:

- CIMs and management presentations
- Data-room files
- Financial models
- Customer diligence notes
- Research links
- IC memo formats
- Deal pipeline logs

CapitalCompass compresses that workflow into one risk-led system.

## Product Modules

- Secure signup/login and admin console
- Premium feature gating and payment-link monetization
- Company material ingestion from decks, documents, spreadsheets, CSV, and notes
- Source URL research and source-quality scoring
- Initial screening note and diligence questions in `.docx`
- PE-grade IC memo in `.pptx`
- Sponsor model in `.xlsx`
- Optional AI API enrichment via OpenAI-compatible, Groq, Anthropic, Gemini, or custom API
- Deal pipeline module with Excel import, manual logging, animated funnel, status mix, sector exposure, and economics charts
- S&P-style acquisition readiness report
